# Design direction — Quant Trading portfolio

1. **Thesis.** The page's product is not a return, it is a *measurement*. The record is negative and the sample is thin; the design's job is to make that legible and credible, so every device is borrowed from instruments and tearsheets rather than from marketing pages.
2. **The gap being filled.** Research desks (AQR, Two Sigma, Jane Street, Bridgewater) publish light, hairline-ruled, tag-and-date research indexes with heavy disclosure apparatus, and none of them are dark. Dark-first product craft (Linear's layered ~#0f1011 surfaces, Vercel's Geist Sans + Geist Mono) has the type discipline but carries no credibility apparatus. This page is AQR's apparatus in a product-grade type system.
3. **Borrowed from Coinbase** (composition and palette): pure-white ground with `#f7f8fa` bands, `#dcdfe4` rules, `#0a0b0d` ink, a cool neutral ramp, generous grid, big unambiguous numbers, and a card-row rhythm for feature blocks. Its `#0052FF` appears here too — but as the *data* colour for a negative value, because Korean market convention is up = red, down = blue. On a losing record that makes Coinbase blue the page's dominant colour honestly rather than decoratively. **Not borrowed:** the single-CTA conversion funnel and the trust badges; this page's "conversion" is a reader believing the numbers, so the equivalent slot holds the paper-trading disclaimer, the sample warnings, and the verdict roll-up.
4. **Borrowed from Linearity** (energy and cadence): an oversized single-line hero at 4–4.5rem with tight tracking, full-bleed neutral bands alternating with white to give the page launch-page rhythm, and scroll-triggered reveals timed at ~520ms on one shared observer. **Not borrowed:** auto-advancing carousels, repeated CTAs, logo walls, and hype copy. Motion here reports, it does not sell — the numbers count up once, the equity line draws in once, nothing loops.
5. **Typographic rule (the signature).** *Mono is what the machine measured; sans is what the human claims.* IBM Plex Mono carries every machine-emitted string — timecodes, tickers, basis points, trip counts, section indices, verdict codes. IBM Plex Sans KR carries prose in both locales. Two families total, self-hosted through `next/font`, no third face.
6. **Structure device: the plate.** Content sits on hairline plates with a brass registration tick in the top-left corner and a mono label in the notch, like a printed instrument panel. Rounded shadow-cards are the templated default and are not used; rules, ticks and bands do the separating.
7. **Section index is real information.** Sections are numbered `01`–`06` because the page *is* a sequence — the record, the per-strategy evidence, the cost that explains its sign, the mechanism, the method, the safeguards — and the sticky rail doubles as a read-progress meter.
8. **Colour discipline.** Data owns red and blue; the accent therefore lives off that axis as a brass ochre (`#8a5e0f` light / `#e0b25f` dark), used only for chrome, eyebrows, ticks and active state, never for a value. No gradients, no glass, no pure black. Light is the default look for every visitor; dark is a tuned, explicit choice the toggle stores, and the OS scheme is deliberately not auto-followed.
9. **Motion is instrumentation.** Numbers count up once on first view; the equity line draws in left-to-right the way a plotter would, in the hero tiles and in section 01; a crosshair follows the pointer with a live axis readout; one pulse dot marks data freshness; blocks rise 14px as they enter. Every one of these is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and the reveal styles only arm once the inline head script marks the document, so a no-JS render is complete rather than blank.
10. **Density and parity.** ~1120px column, 8px rhythm, tabular figures wherever numbers stack. Consumer-fintech subtraction (Toss's whitespace-as-luxury) is explicitly not the target — a research desk should look like it is holding more than fits. Korean and English get the same layout and the same length budget, `word-break: keep-all` stops Korean wrapping mid-word, mono labels stay Latin-only by design, and anything the data cannot support says "설명 준비 중 / Description coming" rather than inventing copy.
